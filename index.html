About

Researcher · Educator · Innovator

Dr. Binju Saju is a dedicated academic researcher with a PhD in Image Processing and Deep Learning from Christ (Deemed to be University), Bangalore. Currently serving as Associate Professor in the Department of AI & Data Science at Adi Shankara Institute of Technology and Engineering, Kalady, she is also a Post-Doctoral Research Fellow at AI-EYS Lab, UK (online mode).

Her research bridges theoretical machine learning with practical applications in Medical Informatics and Agriculture 6.0. With over 50 publications and a Google Scholar H-index of 9, she has developed high-impact models including the Eye-Vision Net for cataract detection and HNRET for CT scan kidney image enhancement.

As an AICTE certified Master Trainer in High-Performance Computing, she brings the technical rigor required for large-scale computational research and cross-disciplinary collaboration. With 11 patents covering AI-aided healthcare devices and smart automation, she is committed to translating research into real-world innovation.
